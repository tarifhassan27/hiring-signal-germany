# Business Problem

When a role sits open for months, it costs the company: lost output, overloaded teams,
and growing pressure to accept lower-quality hires. Yet most companies have no external
reference point to know whether their hiring speed is normal or a real problem.

# The Question

Which role types and which companies in the German tech scale-up space take the longest
to hire — and how do they compare against each other?

# Scope

- German-HQ tech and SaaS companies, 300–2,000 employees
- Data: public job posting feeds (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever)
- Snapshot date: 2026-10-15
- Threshold: 90 days open, calibrated against this cohort (p90 = 51 days across 252 in-scope roles)

# Why this is observable from outside

A job posting's creation date is public. Roles still open after 90+ days are
visible without any internal access. This makes it a measurable proxy for hiring difficulty.
